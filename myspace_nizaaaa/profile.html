Hello, I’m Nurul Aniza!

Hello and welcome to my personal website! My name is Nurul Aniza Ismail Hashim, a passionate and creative Diploma in Information Management student at Universiti Teknologi MARA (UiTM).

I’ve always believed that creativity and technology go hand in hand. ... What started as a small curiosity in HTML and CSS has now turned into a genuine interest in designing and developing user-friendly websites, a journey that has not only taught me the technical skills needed to bring my ideas to life but also helped me discover my passion for creativity and problem-solving. Every project I work on allows me to experiment with layouts, colors, and interactive elements, helping me understand how thoughtful design can improve the user experience. Along the way, I have learned patience, attention to detail, and the joy of seeing a simple concept transform into a functional and visually appealing digital platform that others can enjoy and navigate with ease. This journey continues to inspire me to grow, explore new possibilities, and use technology as a means to express my ideas and personality in meaningful ways.
